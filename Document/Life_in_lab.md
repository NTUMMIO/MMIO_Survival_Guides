### IT Manager

Normally assigned to a 2nd year master’s student. 
Responsibilities include:
1.	Handling server-related incidents and recording them on the GitHub log
2.	Contacting the server vendor for maintenance assistance

5.	Keeping a log of hardware specs, monitor power usage, manage router/IP/remote login

## Life in lab

### Meetings

We normally have weekly lab meetings on Wednesday evenings at 6PM in YL439. The start time may fluctuate during summer and winter breaks but will be announced beforehand by the PI. Meetings are divided into four parts: 1) Progress report: every student records their progress on the Google sheet and reports what they’ve done over the past week. 2) Journal club: each semester every graduate student will share a paper (ideally published within the past 5 years) they’ve read to the lab. Normally one student reports per week; you will sign up in the Lab Schedules Google sheet. In the fall semester, we will have a technical theme for papers and in the spring semester we will have a imaging/clinical theme for papers (to be merged with the Emission Tomography course if the course is offered). 3) Deep dive into results: if needed by the student. 4) General lab-related items for discussion: if needed.

You may bring dinner with you during lab meeting but take out any waste with you after.

Personal meetings are by request.

### Lab space and hardware

For full-time master’s and PhD students, you will receive a cubicle as your personal space in the lab. A desktop computer will be provided for your lab/course work, and will have a GPU installed for local training purposes. For undergraduate students and other personnel, lab space and computers will be granted on a case-by-case basis and depending on availability. USB drives will also be provided upon request; please return upon graduation. Please respect the space and property of your labmates.

### Trash

We do not assign daily monitors to take out the trash in lab, so everyone is responsible for taking out their own trash, especially perishable (food) waste! We do not want actual bugs in our lab either!

### Fridge

The fridge is personal property of the PI. Feel free to use the fridge for temporary food/drink storage. We assign monthly monitors to clean the fridge at the end of the month. Everything that is in the fridge by the last day of the month (default cleaning time: 17:00) will be thrown away. The cleaning time, if changed, will be announced by the monthly monitor at least 24 hours before the scheduled cleaning time on Discord.
